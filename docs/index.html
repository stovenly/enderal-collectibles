Find the collectibles you are still missing in Enderal

Enderal and Forgotten Stories hide five kinds of collectibles. The game keeps a running count of some of them, but it never tells you which ones you have, so tracking down the last few means working through a guide location by location and trying to remember where you have already been.

Load a save here and the page works out which ones you already have. What is left is shown as a list, with the location of each one, and as pins on the world map.

What it tracks

  • 100 Magic Symbols
  • 15 Mystical Symbols
  • 20 Phasmalist Souls
  • 9 Vatyra Fragments
  • 230 Ice Claws

It reads saves from both Enderal (Classic) and Enderal Special Edition, with Forgotten Stories. It all happens in your browser. Your save never leaves your machine.
